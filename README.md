
# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## About the Series

**Stranger Things** is my favorite Netflix series. It is a science fiction, horror, and mystery series created by the Duffer Brothers.

The story takes place in a small town called Hawkins, where a group of friends experience mysterious events and discover a strange world called the *Upside Down*.

## My Favorite Characters

- **Eleven** - A girl with special powers.
- **Mike Wheeler** - One of the main friends.
- **Dustin Henderson** - A funny and intelligent character.
- **Lucas Sinclair** - A brave and loyal friend.
- **Max Mayfield** - A strong and independent character.

## Why I Like Stranger Things

I like this series because it has:
1. Interesting and mysterious stories.
2. Amazing characters and friendships.
3. Exciting adventures.
4. Unexpected twists and suspense.

> "Friends don't lie."
